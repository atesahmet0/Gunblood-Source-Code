# Attribution and provenance

Gunblood was created by **Andrew Wolf / Wolf Games**. Official site: https://www.gunblood.com/.

This is an unofficial preservation and reverse-engineering repository. It contains decompiled/reconstructed ActionScript and extracted assets from the archived Flash game. It is not the original editable `.fla` project, is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creator, and does not claim authorship of the game.

The original game code, graphics, animation, fonts, audio and branding remain the property of their respective rights holders. No open-source license or additional redistribution/modification grant for those materials is asserted by this repository. No license has been applied to the complete collection.

The source SWF was obtained from [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/gunblood_flash). Its exact URL and checksums are in [archive/provenance.json](archive/provenance.json). Availability in an archive is provenance, not a license grant.

The official site's [hosting terms](https://www.gunblood.com/terms_and_conditions.html), retrieved on 2026-10-17, are preserved as [docs/upstream-hosting-terms.html](docs/upstream-hosting-terms.html). They describe requirements concerning developer links and developer advertisements when hosting the game. The local verification harness denies external browser navigation for testing; it is not presented as a deployment configuration satisfying those hosting terms.

The decompilation/validation helper scripts and analyst-assigned names are additions to this recovery. Java/JPEXS tool binaries are not vendored in Git. The GitHub Pages browser runtime is bundled under `docs/ruffle/`, with its Apache and MIT license texts. JPEXS, Eclipse Temurin, and Ruffle retain their own licenses; install them from their official distributions.
